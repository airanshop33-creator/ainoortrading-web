Manajemen & Pimpinan Sourcing Management & Sourcing Lead

Ainoor Trading adalah entitas bisnis berfokus ekspor yang berpusat di Makassar, Sulawesi Selatan. Beroperasi langsung di jantung pusat produksi rumput laut utama Indonesia, kami menjembatani koperasi petani pesisir dengan pembeli internasional yang membutuhkan bahan baku laut berstandar tinggi.

Di bawah kepemimpinan yang terfokus, model bisnis kami mengutamakan pengolahan penjemuran sinar matahari yang bersih, pengontrolan kadar air terstandar, dan transparansi rantai pasok langsung untuk memastikan pabrik ekstraksi karaginan menerima kualitas yang konsisten di setiap pengiriman.

Ainoor Trading is a specialized export-oriented entity headquartered in Makassar, South Sulawesi. Operating directly at the heart of Indonesia's primary seaweed production hub, we bridge coastal farming cooperatives with international buyers demanding high-specification marine raw materials.

Under focused leadership, our business model prioritizes clean solar-drying processing, standardized moisture control, and direct supply chain traceability to ensure carrageenan extraction factories receive consistent quality in every shipment.

Pasokan Langsung Direct Sourcing

Dipanen langsung dari kemitraan koperasi petani pesisir Sulawesi Selatan. Procured directly from South Sulawesi's coastal farming cooperatives.

Kontrol Kualitas Quality Control

Pengujian ketat kadar air (<12% / 32-35%) & kebersihan dari kotoran. Strict moisture (<12% / 32-35%) and impurity screening before baling.

Pusat Logistik Logistics Hub

Pengiriman kontainer FCL langsung via Pelabuhan Makassar (IDUPG). Direct FCL shipping via Port of Makassar (IDUPG).
