About Ainoor Trading

Ainoor Trading is a Makassar-based Indonesian enterprise specialized exclusively in the processing and supply chain management of Eucheuma Cottonii commodities. Operating directly at the coastal sourcing hub of South Sulawesi, we apply rigorous processing standards to transform raw seaweed into high-value export-grade commodities.

From specialized raw material preparation for carrageenan industries to refined organic extracts and industrial sea salt, our focus is delivering standardized Cottonii-derived products that meet global commercial specifications.
