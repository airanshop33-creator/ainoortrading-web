Management & Sourcing Lead

Ainoor Trading is a specialized export-oriented entity headquartered in Makassar, South Sulawesi. Operating directly at the heart of Indonesia's primary seaweed production hub, we bridge coastal farming cooperatives with international buyers demanding high-specification marine raw materials.

Under focused leadership, our business model prioritizes clean solar-drying processing, standardized moisture control, and direct supply chain traceability to ensure carrageenan extraction factories receive consistent quality in every shipment.

Direct Sourcing

Procured directly from South Sulawesi's coastal farming cooperatives.

Quality Control

Strict moisture (<12% / 32-35%) and impurity screening before baling.

Logistics Hub

Direct FCL shipping via Port of Makassar (IDUPG).
