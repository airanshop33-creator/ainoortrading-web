About Ainoor Trading

✓ Fully Registered & Verified by Ministry of Investment/BKPM Indonesia

Ainoor Trading is a Makassar-based Indonesian commodity export firm specializing in high-grade sea commodities. By operating directly at the sourcing origin, we build equitable partnerships with coastal farming cooperatives to ensure consistent quality control and uninterrupted supply chains.

From raw dried seaweed to eco-friendly agricultural inputs, we bridge local coastal production with international commercial requirements.
