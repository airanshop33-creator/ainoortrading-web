About Ainoor Trading

Ainoor Trading is a Makassar-based Indonesian commodity export firm specializing in high-grade marine products. Operating directly at the sourcing origin, we build equitable partnerships with coastal farming cooperatives to ensure consistent quality control and uninterrupted supply chains.

From raw dried seaweed to eco-friendly agricultural inputs and natural sea salt, we bridge local coastal production with international commercial requirements.
